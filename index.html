How to play — Myth or Record?

Explore the 3D graph (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom). Click a node — or pick a name below — and play its card.

Node colours: ● Gold = Patent Edition (Myth or Record?) · ● Sky = Photo Card, 63rd Street (pick the right answer) · ● Paper = Story Check (bonus only — the story itself is not yet verified). Played nodes turn green, red, orange or purple by their published verdict/status.

Then the grade stamp lands, the receipt is shown, and only then points are awarded. Correct call: 100 points. Wrong: 0. The McCoy card is UNVERIFIABLE — call Bonus for 50.

Digital solo scoring is a stated adaptation: the live show scores by evidence grade (VERIFIED 100 / SUPPORTED 75 / PROVISIONAL 50), but these Episode 1 cards carry published verdicts, not those grades — so solo play scores flat, and says so.

About Prove It! — FAQ & card index

What is Prove It!?

Prove It! is a free Afro-American and African diaspora game by CultureTechLens NFP, a Chicago 501(c)(3) Black cultural intelligence archive. You play inside a Three.js 3D knowledge graph: click a node, answer the card, and read the receipt — every answer carries its published evidence grade and source. Don't just know it. Prove it.

How do you play?

Gold Patent Edition nodes ask Myth or Record? — call the claim about a Black inventor, then check the U.S. patent record. Sky-blue Photo Card nodes ask multiple-choice questions about Cited Photo Archive photographs of 63rd Street, Chicago (White City amusement park, the Trianon Ballroom, John H. White's 1973 EPA photographs). Paper Story Check nodes are bonus-only. 21 cards, up to 1,950 points.

What are the evidence grades?

CultureTechLens grades claims VERIFIED · SUPPORTED · PROVISIONAL · DISPUTED · UNRESOLVED · REFUTED · UNVERIFIABLE — never upgraded for presentation. Patent verdicts are stamped FALSE / EXAGGERATED / CONFIRMED / MYTH exactly as published.

What is CTL-MEDUSA?

CTL-MEDUSA is the register prefix of the CultureTechLens Cited Photo Archive. Photo Cards carry register IDs such as CTL-MEDUSA-CHI-000001-001 (White City construction, c. 1905), CTL-MEDUSA-CHI-000001-002 (White City at night, 1916), CTL-MEDUSA-CHI-000001-003 (Trianon Ballroom, 1924) and CTL-MEDUSA-CHI-000001-005 (63rd Street, July 1973). All photographs are Public Domain; the game links to the archive instead of republishing them.
